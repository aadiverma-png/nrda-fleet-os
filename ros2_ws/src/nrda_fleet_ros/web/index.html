            How the probabilities are worked out

            Deadlock. Robots are linked when one waits for another. A closed loop of waits is a deadlock: 100%. Otherwise the chance grows with the longest standing wait T and the number of robots in the chain n: P = (1 − e−T/25 s) × min(1, 0.5 + 0.25(n − 1)).

            Collision. Every pair of robots within 6 m is projected ahead at its current speed for 3 s. From the closest approach d at time t, the margin is m = d − 0.9 m and the uncertainty σ = 0.12 + 0.1t m, so P = Φ(−m/σ). The robots use the same model on board to brake early.

            Solved. A deadlock is ticked when the robot given priority gets through, or when every robot in it is moving again. A collision is ticked once both robots have separated and driven on. Near misses are ticked at once: the robots stopped in time.
